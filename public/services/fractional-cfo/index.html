Fractional CFO

Fractional CFO services for San Jose businesses

A fractional CFO is a part-time chief financial officer.

(408) 898-1701Contact us

TB Accounting Services in San Jose provides fractional CFO services to businesses that need budgets, cash flow forecasts, financing support and financial planning, but are not ready for a full-time CFO. You get CFO guidance for the hours your business needs.

Free first consultation. Remote or in person, from our San Jose office.

Is it time

Is your business ready for a fractional CFO?

A fractional CFO makes sense when the decisions are getting bigger than the bookkeeping. These are the usual signs.

  • Sales are growing, but cash always seems tight.
  • You are planning a loan, an investment, a big purchase or a sale of the business.
  • You need a budget your team can actually follow.
  • You make every financial decision alone, and you want a second view.
  • You do not know which products, jobs or clients make the most money.

The work

What does a fractional CFO do for your business?

A fractional CFO takes accurate numbers and turns them into plans. This is the work we cover.

Budgeting

An annual budget built with you, then compared with actual results each month.

Cash flow forecasting

A forecast of the cash coming in and going out over the coming months, so a shortfall is visible early.

Financial planning and strategy

Guidance on pricing, hiring, spending and growth, grounded in your own numbers.

Loan and financing support

Financial statements, forecasts and documents prepared for lenders and investors.

Mergers and acquisitions

Help getting ready to buy or sell a business, from records to review.

Custom reports and dashboards

The handful of numbers you need to watch, reported in a way you can read at a glance.

Start with the basics

Why does CFO work start with accurate books?

A forecast is only as good as the numbers underneath it.

If your books are behind or unreliable, we fix that first. Because our firm also handles bookkeeping and the monthly close, your CFO works from records we already know.

How it runs

How does a fractional CFO engagement work?

Every business is different, so the rhythm is set with you. Most engagements follow these steps.

  2. A look at the numbers

Questions

What do owners ask about fractional CFO services?

How is a fractional CFO different from a full-time CFO?

A fractional CFO does the same kind of work, such as budgets, forecasts and financial planning, but works with your business part time. You pay for the time you need instead of a full-time executive salary.

Can a fractional CFO help us get a loan?

We help prepare the financial statements, forecasts and documents lenders usually ask for, and support you through the loan filing. The lender makes the final decision.

Do you help with buying or selling a business?

Yes. We assist with mergers and acquisitions, such as getting the financial records ready, reviewing the numbers and supporting you through the process.

How much time will the CFO spend with us?

That is set with you at the start, based on what your business needs. It can change as the business grows or as a big decision comes up.

Can you work with our current bookkeeper or accountant?

Yes. A fractional CFO can work from books kept by your own team or another firm. If the books need work first, we can handle that too.
