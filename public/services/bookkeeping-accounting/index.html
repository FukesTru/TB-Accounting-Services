Bookkeeping and accounting services in San Jose

TB Accounting Services provides bookkeeping and accounting for businesses from its office in downtown San Jose, California. The basics

What is the difference between bookkeeping and accounting?

Bookkeeping is the daily recording of every sale, bill and payment. Accounting uses those records to produce financial statements, check them for errors and explain what they mean.

We do both. That means the people reading your numbers are close to the people keeping them, and problems get caught early.

BookkeepingRecording income, expenses and payments as they happen, and keeping every account reconciled
AccountingTurning those records into monthly financial statements and explaining what they show

Every month

What does TB Accounting Services handle each month?

Our outsourced bookkeeping and accounting covers the routine work that keeps your records accurate and up to date.

  • Bookkeeping and data entry. Every sale, bill and payment recorded in your accounting software.
  • Accounts payable. The money you owe vendors, tracked so bills are paid on time.
  • Accounts receivable. The money customers owe you, tracked so nothing is missed.
  • Invoicing and collections. Invoices sent and follow-up on unpaid balances.
  • Payroll processing. Employees and contractors paid, with payroll recorded correctly.
  • Reconciliations. Bank, credit card and loan accounts matched to their statements.
  • Cash flow management. A clear view of the cash coming in and going out.
  • Monthly financial reports. Clear reports each month, plus year-end reporting for your taxes.

Financial cleanup

What if your books are months behind?

General ledger cleanup brings messy or out of date books back to accurate and current. The general ledger is the master record of every transaction in your business.

  • General ledger review and cleanup. We find and correct errors and miscoded entries.
  • Account reconciliation. Every account is matched to its statements, period by period.
  • Temporary account resolution. We clear out the holding accounts where unclear items were parked.

Before we start

What should you have ready before we start?

Gathering these before the first meeting saves time. If something is missing, we can work around it.

  • Bank, credit card and loan statements for the months that need work
  • Access to your accounting software, if you use one
  • Payroll reports and a list of employees and contractors
  • Open invoices you have sent and bills you have not yet paid
  • Your most recent business tax return
  • Any notes from a previous bookkeeper or accountant

Monthly reporting

Which reports show how your business is doing?

Each month the books produce a set of financial statements. These are the main ones, in plain words.

Profit and loss statement

Shows your income and expenses for the month and the year so far, and whether the business made a profit.

Balance sheet

Shows what the business owns, what it owes and what is left for the owners on a given date.

Cash flow view

Shows where cash came from and where it went, so a profitable month with an empty bank account makes sense.

Questions

What do business owners ask about bookkeeping?

How far behind can my books be?

We start with a review of the general ledger to see how far back the work goes. Then we reconcile each account, month by month, until the books are current. The free consultation tells you what that involves for your business.

Can you run payroll?

Yes. Payroll processing is part of our bookkeeping and accounting service, alongside payables, receivables and monthly reporting.

Will I get monthly reports?

Yes. We deliver financial reports each month, so you can see income, costs and cash without waiting for tax season.

What is a reconciliation?

A reconciliation matches the transactions in your books to your bank or credit card statement, line by line. It catches missing entries, duplicates and errors before they reach your financial statements or your tax return.

Do I need to be in San Jose?

No. We work with businesses across the United States and abroad remotely in real time, and meet in person when needed.
