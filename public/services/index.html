Running your business

Which services keep your business finances in order?

These 4 services work on their own or together. Many clients start with bookkeeping and add controller, CFO or tax work as the business grows.

Bookkeeping & Accounting

Monthly bookkeeping, reconciliations, payables, receivables and payroll, with clear financial reports each month.

Controller Services

A steady month-end close, accurate financial statements and the controls that keep your records right.

Fractional CFO

Tax Services

Business tax preparation and filing built on reconciled books, plus planning before the year ends.

When money is in question

Which services help with disputes, trusts and missing money?

These services start with a question about the money. Business owners, trustees, families and attorneys can ask us about any of them on a free consultation.

Forensic Accounting

Forensic accounting means reviewing financial records closely to find, measure and explain what happened, usually for a dispute or a legal matter. We report what we find in plain language.

Read about Forensic Accounting

Financial Investigations

A financial investigation follows money through accounts and records to show where it came from and where it went. It suits cases where funds are missing, misused or hard to follow.

Read about Financial Investigations

Trust Accounting

Trust accounting keeps exact records of money and property held for someone else. We prepare trust accountings and records for trustees, families and the attorneys who advise them.

Read about Trust Accounting

Expert Witness Services

When a legal matter turns on financial records, attorneys can ask us about financial analysis, written reports and support for testimony. We report what the records show.

Read about Expert Witness Services
