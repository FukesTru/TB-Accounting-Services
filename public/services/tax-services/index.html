Business returns

Which business tax returns do we prepare?

Most businesses file a single federal return from this list, plus the California return that goes with it.

Partnerships and multi-member LLCs

Federal Form 1065. A partnership passes its income through to its partners, who each receive a Schedule K-1 for their own return.

S corporations

Federal Form 1120-S. An S corporation is a corporation that passes its income through to its shareholders instead of paying most federal income tax itself.

C corporations

Federal Form 1120. A C corporation pays tax on its own profits, separate from its owners.

California business returns

The state returns that match your federal filing, for businesses that operate in California.

Key dates

When are business tax returns due?

These are the general federal dates for a business on a calendar year. When a date falls on a weekend or holiday, it moves to the next business day.

March 15Partnership and S corporation federal returns are generally due by March 15 for a calendar-year business.
April 15C corporation federal returns are generally due by April 15 for a calendar-year business.
ExtensionsAn extension gives more time to file the return, not more time to pay the tax owed.
$800 in CaliforniaMost LLCs and corporations doing business in California owe an annual minimum tax of $800 to the state.

Dates and amounts change, and some businesses use a fiscal year. We confirm the deadlines that apply to your business when we take on the work. This page is general information, not tax advice.

Before your return

What should you gather for your business tax return?

Having these ready makes the return faster and more accurate. If we keep your books, we already have most of them.

  • Year-end financial statements, including the profit and loss statement and balance sheet
  • Bank, credit card and loan statements through the last day of the year
  • Payroll reports, plus W-2 and 1099 information for the people you paid
  • Records of equipment, vehicles or property bought or sold during the year
  • Last year's federal and California returns
  • Records of estimated tax payments made during the year

Year-end planning

How does tax planning before year end help?

Planning in December gives you choices. Filing in March only records what already happened.

Year-end tax planning looks at your results while the year is still open. That leaves time to think about the timing of purchases, estimated payments and how income and expenses are recorded.

We cannot promise a lower tax bill, because that depends on your facts and the law. What planning does give you is fewer surprises and a return built on numbers you have already seen.

Questions

What do business owners ask about tax services?

Can you prepare our return if someone else keeps our books?

Tell us who keeps your books now. We review the records first, and that review shows whether anything needs fixing before the return is prepared.

What if we are behind on filing?

Contact us as soon as you can. We start by getting the books current, then prepare the returns that are due. Late filing and late payment can each carry penalties, so it helps to act early.

Do you prepare California returns?

Yes. We prepare California business returns along with the federal returns, and can talk with you about other states where your business may need to file.

What is a tax extension?

An extension gives your business more time to file its return. It does not give more time to pay. Tax that is owed is still due by the original deadline.

Can you help us plan for next year?

Yes. Year-end tax planning looks at your results before December 31, while there is still time to make choices about timing, estimated payments and records.
