About TB Accounting Services

TB Accounting Services, LLC is an accounting and bookkeeping firm in downtown San Jose, California, owned by Robert Carrillo. We provide bookkeeping, controller, fractional CFO, tax, forensic and trust accounting services to clients across the United States and abroad, remotely in real time or in person when needed.

How we work

How do we work with clients?

We adapt to how your business runs, so working with us feels like having your own finance team.

  • We serve clients remotely in real time, and in person when needed.
  • We deliver financial reports each month and explain what they show.
  • We take on long-term work and short-term projects.
  • We answer client questions promptly and give clear guidance.

Our aim

What are we here to do?

To make your finances clear, so you can focus on running and growing your business.

Accurate records come first. Clear reporting comes next. Then the advice that helps you decide what to do with the numbers.
