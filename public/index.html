Questions

What do clients ask before they call?

Short answers to the questions we hear most. Call us for anything not covered here.

(408) 898-1701

Is the first consultation really free?

Yes. The first consultation with TB Accounting Services is free. We use it to learn what you need, look at where your records stand and explain how we would handle the work.

Do you only work with businesses in San Jose?

No. TB Accounting Services is based in downtown San Jose, California, and works with clients across the United States and in other countries. We work remotely in real time and meet in person when needed.

Can you catch up books that are months behind?

Yes. We review and clean up the general ledger, reconcile each account and clear out balances that do not belong. The general ledger is the master record of every transaction in your business. Once it is current, we can keep it current each month.

What does a fractional CFO do?

A fractional CFO is a chief financial officer who works with your business part time. The role covers budgets, cash flow forecasts, financing and planning for the hours your business needs, instead of a full-time hire.

Do you prepare business tax returns?

Yes. Our tax services cover business tax preparation and filing, plus planning before the year ends. When we also keep your books, your tax work starts from records we already know.
