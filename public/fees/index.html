The free consultation

What happens in the free consultation?

It is a working conversation about your business. You leave it knowing what needs doing and what it would cost.

  1. We learn about your business

    What you do, how big the business is, who keeps the books now and which software you use.

  2. We look at where the records stand

    How current the books are, how many accounts there are and what deadlines are coming up.

  3. We explain what the work involves

    Which service fits, what we would do and how often, in plain words.

  4. You get the fee

    We explain the fee before any work starts. There is no obligation to go ahead.

What sets the price

What affects the fee for accounting work?

These are the things we look at when we quote. Knowing them helps you have the right papers ready for the consultation.

How many accounts you have

The number of bank, credit card and loan accounts, and how many transactions run through them each month.

How current your books are

Books that are up to date take less work than books that need months of catch-up and reconciliation.

Payroll and people

Whether we run payroll, and how many employees and contractors are paid.

How often you want reports

Monthly reporting, quarterly reviews or year-end only, plus any custom reports you need.

How complex the business is

Several entities, locations or project-based jobs add steps to the work.

Matter work

For forensic, investigation, trust and expert witness work, the volume of records and the reports or testimony needed.

Ongoing work and projects

How are fees set for ongoing work and for projects?

We explain which applies to your work, and why, before you decide.

Ongoing work

Bookkeeping and accounting, controller services and fractional CFO work run every month. They are set up as regular, recurring work with a clear scope.

Projects

A ledger cleanup, a business tax return, a trust accounting, a forensic review or an investigation is scoped as a project with its own fee.

Questions

What do clients ask about fees?

Do you charge for the first consultation?

No. The first consultation with TB Accounting Services is free, and there is no obligation to hire us afterward.

Why are there no set prices on this website?

The work varies too much between clients for a single price to be useful. A business with a single bank account and current books needs far less work than a business with several entities and a year of catch-up. We quote after we understand your situation.

Will the fee change if my books are behind?

Catch-up work is usually quoted on its own, separate from the ongoing monthly work. We explain both before anything starts, so you know what each part costs.

Will I know the fee before work starts?

Yes. We explain the scope and the fee before any work starts. You decide whether to go ahead.
