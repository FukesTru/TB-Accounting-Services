Privacy

Privacy policy

This privacy policy explains what information TB Accounting Services, LLC collects through www.tbacct.com, how we use it and the choices you have. TB Accounting Services, LLC is an accounting firm at 66 E Santa Clara St, Ste 7, San Jose, CA 95113.

Last updated November 13, 2026

What information do we collect?

We collect the information you choose to give us. When you send the contact form, that is your name, your phone number and your message. When you use the chat, it is what you type and any contact details you share. When you call or email us, it is your phone number or email address and what you tell us.

Like most websites, our hosting provider records basic technical details when you visit, such as your browser type, the pages you view and your IP address. We use these records to keep the website working and secure.

How do we use your information?

  • To reply to your enquiry and talk with you about our services.
  • To set up and hold a consultation you asked for.
  • To keep records of our conversations with you.
  • To keep this website working, secure and easy to use.

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it with anyone for their own marketing.

Will we contact you by text message?

Yes, if you give us your phone number. When you send the contact form or start a chat, you agree that TB Accounting Services may contact you by phone call or text message about your enquiry. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply.

You can stop text messages at any time by replying STOP. Reply HELP for help, or call us at (408) 898-1701. Mobile numbers and text message consent are not shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing purposes.

Which services handle your information?

Our contact form and chat are provided by LeadConnector, a customer messaging platform. Information you send through them is stored on that platform so our team can reply. The website is hosted by Vercel. These providers handle information for us under their own security and privacy terms.

Do we use cookies?

This website itself does not use advertising or tracking cookies. The contact form and chat may use cookies or similar storage so they work properly, for example to keep a chat conversation open as you move between pages. Your browser settings let you block or delete cookies, though the chat may not work fully if you do.

The website remembers in your browser that you have seen the opening animation, so it does not play again on every page. This is stored only for your current browser session.

How long do we keep information?

We keep enquiry information for as long as we need it to respond to you and to keep reasonable business records. If you become a client, your engagement and records are covered by the terms we agree with you.

How do we protect information?

We use reasonable steps to protect the information you give us. No website or online service is completely secure, so please do not send account numbers, Social Security numbers, tax documents or other sensitive details through the form or chat. We will arrange a secure way to share documents if you become a client.

What choices do you have?

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it or ask us to delete it. California residents may have additional rights under California law. To make a request, email info@tbacct.com or call (408) 898-1701. We will not treat you differently for making a request.

Children

This website is meant for adults and businesses. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
