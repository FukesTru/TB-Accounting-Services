For trustees

What a trustee should keep, and why accountings are prepared

Under California Probate Code section 16062, a trustee generally must account to the trust's beneficiaries at least once a year, when the trust ends and when there is a change of trustee. Some exceptions apply, so the trust attorney should confirm which rules fit your trust.

Records worth keeping from day one

  • The trust document and any amendments
  • A list of what the trust held at the start
  • Bank and brokerage statements for every trust account
  • Receipts and invoices for expenses the trust paid
  • Records of distributions to beneficiaries
  • Property records, such as deeds, leases and rent rolls

Why the accounting matters

A trust accounting tells the story of the trust's money for a set period, from the opening balance to the closing balance. It shows what came in, what went out, what was distributed and what the trust holds at the end.

Beneficiaries use it to understand how the trust is being managed. A complete, well-supported accounting also protects the trustee, because every figure can be traced back to a record.

Questions

What do Campbell clients ask us?

How often does a California trustee need to account to beneficiaries?

California law generally requires a trustee to account to beneficiaries at least once a year, when the trust ends and when there is a change of trustee. Some exceptions apply, so ask the trust attorney which rules fit your trust.

What records should a trustee keep?

Keep the trust document, a list of what the trust held at the start, statements for every trust account, receipts for expenses, records of distributions and property records. We can work from partial records and tell you what else to request.

What if family members disagree about the trust records?

When an accounting is questioned, the records may need a closer look. Our forensic accounting and financial investigation services can help trace and document what happened.

Do you keep the books for small shops and practices in Campbell?

Yes. Retail shops and professional practices use our bookkeeping and accounting service for monthly books, payroll, reconciliations and reports.
