Beyond the valley

How do clients across the US and abroad work with us?

Most accounting work does not need a meeting room. Clients outside Santa Clara County work with us remotely in real time, by phone, email and online.

The work is the same wherever you are. We keep the books, close the month, prepare reports and answer questions as they come up. When a matter calls for a meeting in person, we arrange one.

  • The same 8 services, whether you are in San Jose or another state
  • Monthly financial reports, explained in plain words
  • Prompt answers by phone and email during office hours
  • In-person meetings at our downtown office when the work needs it

What do clients ask about where we work?

Do you have to be in San Jose to work with us?

No. TB Accounting Services is based in downtown San Jose, California, and works with clients across the United States and in other countries. We work remotely in real time and meet in person when needed.

Can we meet in person?

Yes. Our office is at 66 E Santa Clara St, Ste 7, in downtown San Jose. Call ahead so we can set a time. We also work remotely in real time if that suits you better.

Yes. We work with clients across the United States and in other countries, mostly remotely, and meet in person when the work needs it.
