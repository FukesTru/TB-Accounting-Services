The California layer

Which state agencies does a California business deal with?

Besides the IRS, a California business usually deals with state agencies too. Which ones apply depends on what the business does and whether it has employees.

Franchise Tax Board

The FTB handles California income and franchise tax for businesses and their owners.

Department of Tax and Fee Administration

The CDTFA handles sales and use tax for businesses that sell taxable goods in California.

Employment Development Department

The EDD handles payroll tax reporting for California employers.

Each agency asks for figures that come out of your books, such as income, sales and wages. When the books are reconciled every month, those figures are ready and they agree with each other, which makes every filing easier. Ask us which of these apply to your business on a free consultation. This is general information, not tax advice.

Questions

What do Sunnyvale clients ask us?

Which state tax agencies does a Sunnyvale business deal with?

Many California businesses deal with the Franchise Tax Board (FTB) for income and franchise tax, the Department of Tax and Fee Administration (CDTFA) for sales and use tax, and the Employment Development Department (EDD) for payroll. Which ones apply depends on your business.

Can you run payroll for a small Sunnyvale business?

Yes. Payroll processing is part of our bookkeeping and accounting service, alongside payables, receivables, reconciliations and monthly reporting.

When are business tax returns due?

For a business on a calendar year, partnership and S corporation federal returns are generally due March 15, and C corporation returns are generally due April 15. We confirm the dates that apply to you.

Do owner-run businesses and professionals work with you?

Yes. Owner-run businesses and professional practices use the same bookkeeping and tax services as larger companies, set up for the size of the work.
